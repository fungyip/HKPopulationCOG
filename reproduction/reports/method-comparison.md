# Method comparison

Controlled comparison of the legacy calculation against the revised method, to
isolate which change causes any difference in the result. Generated by
`reproduction/scripts/compare_methods.py`.

## Methods

| Method | Change vs legacy | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `legacy` (A) | None | Reproduce the original |
| `projected` (B) | Coordinate system only (HK 1980 Grid, EPSG:2326) | Isolate effect of degree-vs-projected averaging |
| `projected_coverage_corrected` (D) | Projected + remove geocoding error | Isolate effect of coverage/error treatment |

Representative-location comparison (C: name-geocoded points vs polygon
centroids) is deferred to the HKGeo work, because it requires matching
2001/2011 area names to the 2015 boundary shapefile.

## Shift 2001 → 2011 by method

| method | distance | bearing |
|---|---|---|
| legacy | 5,388.7 m | 134.5° |
| projected | 5,322.4 m | 138.1° |
| projected_coverage_corrected | **480.6 m** | **277.8°** |

## Interpretation

1. **The coordinate system is a second-order effect.** On clean 2011 data, the
   projected (EPSG:2326) mean centre differs from the degree average by only
   **2.5 m**. The "direct latitude/longitude averaging" limitation is real but
   negligible at Hong Kong's scale.

2. **Projection does not fix bad data.** On the raw 2001 data, the projected
   centre differs from legacy by 347 m — *more*, not less — because the
   "North - Yan Do" point (Sichuan, China) projects to a nonsense HK 1980 Grid
   coordinate and is amplified by the transverse Mercator distortion.

3. **The dominant error is the geocoding outlier.** Removing "North - Yan Do"
   moves the 2001 centre by ~5.78 km, collapsing the 2001→2011 shift from
   ~5.4 km to **~481 m (bearing ~278°, essentially due west)**.

## Conclusion

Any change from the original result is fully attributable to a traceable input
or method change. The revised pipeline (clean → project → weighted mean) yields
a trustworthy baseline for the HKGeo interactive upgrade:

- 2011 centre: (114.1516, 22.3533)
- 2001 centre (coverage-corrected): (114.1562, 22.3528)
- Shift: ~481 m due west (not ~5.5 km southward)
